Diggo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who we are

Diggo is a mobile app to discover vinyl records from independent record shops, listen to previews and buy from those shops. The data controller is Francesco Villa, operating diggo (“diggo”, “we”).

For any privacy question or request write to diggoadmin@gmail.com.

4. Who we share it with

Record shops. When you send a purchase request, the shop receives your contact email, your username, the records, your delivery or pick-up choice and your note, so it can reply to you directly and complete the sale. When you check out, you are sent to the shop's own checkout page. From that moment the shop handles your order and payment as an independent data controller under its own privacy policy. The shops are the ones shown in the app (today: Vinylminded, Italy).

Common Ground. Some shops sell through the Common Ground platform. When you check out with one of them, diggo creates the checkout with the records you chose and afterwards checks only whether that checkout is still open. We do not send Common Ground your email or name; you enter your details on the shop's checkout page.

Some providers above are based in the United States or may process data there. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • access your data and get a copy;
  • correct it (you can change your username yourself in your profile);
  • delete it: use Profile → Delete account in the app, or write to us;
  • restrict its use or receive it in a portable format;
  • object to its use based on legitimate interest, such as usage statistics;
  • withdraw consent at any time (for notifications: your phone settings), without affecting what happened before.

Write to diggoadmin@gmail.com; we reply within one month. If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with the authority of the EU country where you live.

8. Children, security, changes

Children. diggo is not meant for people under 14 years of age. If you are under that age, do not create an account.

Security. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the database is limited by per-user rules, so each account can read only its own data; backups are encrypted.

Changes. If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will tell you in the app before the change applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
