Diggo Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Information We Collect

Diggo collects the following information when you use our app:

  • Email address and username (for account creation)
  • Music preferences (liked and disliked records)
  • Session data (music discovery sessions)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the Diggo service
  • Connect you with local record shops
  • Send you relevant notifications about your requests

Third Party Services

Diggo uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase (database and authentication)
  • Google Sign In
  • Apple Sign In
  • Firebase (push notifications)

Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can request account deletion by emailing diggoadmin@gmail.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at: diggoadmin@gmail.com
